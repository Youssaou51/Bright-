## Frequently Asked Questions

### Account Management

**How do I create an account?**
1. Open the Bright Future app
2. Tap "Sign Up"
3. Enter your email, username, and password
4. Verify your email address

**How do I delete my account?**
1. Go to your Profile
2. Scroll down and tap "Delete Account"
3. Confirm the deletion
4. Your account and all data will be permanently removed



### Using the App

**How do I post content?**
1. Tap the "+" button on the home screen
2. Choose to take a photo/video or select from gallery
3. Add a caption
4. Tap "Post"

**How do I edit my profile?**
1. Go to your Profile tab
2. Tap the edit icon
3. Update your information
4. Save changes



### Privacy & Safety

**Is my data secure?**
Yes, we use industry-standard encryption and security practices to protect your data.

**How do I report inappropriate content?**

**Can I control who sees my posts?**
Currently, all posts are visible to the community. Private posts are coming in a future update.

## Technical Support

### App Issues

**The app crashes when I try to take a photo**
- Make sure you've granted camera permissions in Settings
- Try restarting the app
- If the issue persists, use the gallery option instead

**I can't upload photos/videos**
- Check your internet connection
- Ensure the file size is under 50MB
- Try a different file format

**The app won't load**
- Check your internet connection
- Force close and restart the app
- Clear the app cache in your device settings
